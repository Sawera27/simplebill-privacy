Privacy Policy

App: Simple Bill  |  Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Android app Simple Bill (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information. In short: Simple Bill does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data.

1. What the app does

Simple Bill lets you type your shop or personal name, the name of the person or shop you are billing, and a list of items with prices and quantities. It calculates the total and lets you share the bill as a picture or as text.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any information. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party SDKs that gather data. The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device on its own.

3. Information stored on your device

  • Your shop / personal name and currency symbol: saved locally on your device so you don’t have to type them again. This data never leaves your device.
  • Bill details (customer name, items, prices, quantities): kept only while the app is in use and are not sent to us or stored on any server.
  • Temporary bill picture: when you choose to share a bill as a picture, the app creates an image file in its own private cache. It is replaced the next time you share and is removed when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app.

4. Sharing

The app shares a bill only when you tap a share button. The bill is then handed to the app you choose in the Android share menu (for example WhatsApp, SMS, or email). That app’s own privacy policy governs how it handles the bill. We never receive a copy.

5. Permissions

Simple Bill requests no Android permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or phone identifiers.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general business tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

7. Data deletion

Because all data stays on your device, you control it. You can delete your saved shop name and currency by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Security

Data saved by the app is kept in the app’s private storage area, which other apps cannot normally access, and app backups are disabled. No data is transmitted by the app, so there is no network exposure.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new “Last updated” date.
